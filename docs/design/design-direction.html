Hero concept: a stack of working papers on a ledger grid. The orange stroke is an audit tick mark, the same mark as the check in SCO's logo, drawn once as if by the reviewer's pen. The figures on the paper are illustrative; the live site shows no numbers until the firm confirms them. Built in SVG and CSS rather than WebGL, so it adds about 3 KB and stays still under reduced motion.

01 · Typeface candidates

Three pairings, one recommendation

All candidates are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so they are free for commercial use and can be self-hosted through next/font. I set aside Cairo, Tajawal and Almarai as too common. A premium commercial Arabic face (such as 29LT or Monotype) would need a paid web licence for every domain and adds little for this brief.

02 · Specimen A

Type scale, prose, numbers, table, form

Arabic body text is set at 18px with a line height of 1.85 and no letter-spacing. Emphasis uses weight or colour, never italics. English body text is 17px at 1.6. Display sizes use a 1.25 ratio and are fluid with clamp().

03 · Palette

Taken from the logo, then disciplined

The logo pairs ultramarine #04019F with a signal orange #FC4E08. Blue carries authority: headings, links and primary buttons. Orange is the one accent, kept to the tick mark, rules, key figures and focus rings. Neutrals lean slightly blue so the page reads as cool paper, not grey. Use the theme switch at the top to see the dark palette; contrast ratios are measured against the page background.

Rule: brand orange on a light background is 3.1:1, which passes only for large text (24px and up) and graphics. Small orange text uses --orange-text (#C23A00, 5.0:1). Primary buttons use white on blue (13.9:1). On dark backgrounds the orange is lifted to #FF6A2B (6.9:1).

04 · Motion & detail

Precise, slow, never noisy

  • One signature momentThe audit tick draws on the hero, and on "verified" items such as credentials and completed steps. It is used nowhere else.
  • Ledger linesHairline rules draw from the start edge as sections enter. In RTL they draw from the right.
  • Headings reveal by wordArabic is split by word, never by letter, so the joined letterforms stay intact. Each word rises 8px and fades in over 600ms with a 40ms stagger.
  • Real figures onlyCounters animate once, using tabular figures, and only when the firm has confirmed the values. Otherwise the block is hidden.
  • Lenis smooth scroll + GSAP ScrollTriggerLazy-loaded after the first paint. With prefers-reduced-motion everything renders in its final state.
  • Micro-interactionsButtons lift 1px and their arrow nudges toward the reading direction. Client logos go from greyscale to colour on hover or focus. Page transitions are a 200ms cross-fade.
